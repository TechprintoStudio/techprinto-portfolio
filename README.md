## About Techprinto Studio

**Techprinto Studio** is a software development and technology solutions company founded by **Vasantharaj Balamurugan**, based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India.

We transform innovative ideas into scalable digital products through modern engineering, creative design, and intelligent automation.
